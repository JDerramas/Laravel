# 🚀 Engineering Daily Devlog — September 19, 2026

## 🏛️ PHASE 12: NPC ARCHITECTURAL CAD BLUEPRINT & BLENDER 3D BIM WORKSTATION DEPLOYMENT

### 1. Overview & Project Motivation
Upang maiangat ang Navotas Polytechnic College (NPC) sa modernong digital campus standard, binuo ang isang state-of-the-art **Campus 3D BIM & 2D CAD Architectural Workstation** (`campus_map.php`). Ang layunin nito ay:
1. **Interactive Spatial Wayfinding**: Tulungan ang mga estudyante at guro na mahanap ang kanilang mga silid-aralan, laboratoryo, at opisina ng mga propesor.
2. **Dual-Engine Visualization**:
   - **3D Blender BIM Mode**: Real procedural 3D model na may dynamic floor slicing (dollhouse effect), orbit navigation, at first-person walkthrough.
   - **2D AutoCAD Blueprint Mode**: Professional vector floor plans na may DPWH column grid (A–J, 1–10), structural dimensions, door swings, at live crosshair measuring tape.
3. **Live Academic Synchronization**: I-link ang bawat kwarto sa live database schedules upang makita kung sino ang kasalukuyang nagtuturong propesor at kung available o in-use ang kwarto.

---

### 2. Implementation Architecture

#### A. Blender 5.2 Procedural Model Pipeline (`a.py`)
- Nilikha ang procedural generation script gamit ang Blender 5.2 `bmesh` API:
  - **Super-Expanded Footprint (76m × 76m)**: Malawakang pinalaki ang floor slabs mula 56m tungong **76.0m × 76.0m** upang maluwag na magkasya ang lahat ng classrooms, laboratories, at corridors nang walang clipping.
  - **Dedicated Solid 4F Slab para sa Gymnasium**:
    - Ang 4F floor slab ay binuo bilang isang **solid slab** (walang central void) upang ang **42m × 30m Multi-Purpose Gymnasium Arena** (`4F-GYM`, 1,260 m²) ay buong-buong nakapatong sa concrete slab.
    - Walang overhang o overflow sa atrium; may 5-tier spectator bleachers, player benches, scoring table, at FIBA regulation hoops.
  - **Vibrant Wall Coloring Palette ("lagyan mo kulay yung mga pader")**:
    - **Academic Navy Accent Walls (`#0a1e3f`)**: Front whiteboard/screen walls sa classrooms.
    - **Warm Sand Cream Walls (`#f1ede4`)**: Maaliwalas na interior walls.
    - **Tech Slate & Blue (`#1e293b` & `#0284c7`)**: High-tech ambiance sa computer labs.
    - **Aqua Glazed Teal (`#0d9488`)**: Sanitary tile finish sa lahat ng comfort rooms.
    - **Sports Oak Wainscoting & Navy Upper Bands**: Gym arena perimeter walls.
    - **Institutional Navy Columns (`#001736`)**: Matitibay na poste sa 10×10 column grid (Grids A hanggang J, 1 hanggang 10 sa 8.0m spacing).
  - **Headless Blender Export**: Na-compile at na-export gamit ang headless command `D:\blender.exe --background --python a.py` tungo sa `assets/models/npc_campus_blender.glb` (~3.50 MB).

#### B. Three.js 3D Client Engine (`assets/js/npc-blender-3d.js`)
- Isinulat ang dedicated class `NpcBlender3D` na may:
  - Custom camera transitions (`lerp` animations) papunta sa piniling silid.
  - `setFloor(floorId)`: Slicing algorithm na nagtatago ng mga upper floor slabs upang mabuksan ang loob ng napiling palapag.
  - `isWalkMode`: First-person controller na sumusuporta sa WASD + Mouse PointerLock navigation para sa interactive walking tour.

#### C. 2D AutoCAD Blueprint Engine (`assets/js/npc-campus-cad.js`)
- Isinulat ang class `NpcCadEngine` na nagpapakita ng:
  - Architectural vector blueprint na may DPWH grid system.
  - Clickable room boundary polygons na may instant highlighting.
  - Built-in measuring tool na nagkakalkula ng distansya sa metro sa bawat drag ng mouse.

#### D. Live Spatial REST API (`api/campus_map.php`)
- Nilikha ang backend endpoint na nagbabalik ng:
  - `action=get_all`: Lahat ng 35+ rooms, active timetable data, live room availability (`available` vs `occupied`), at faculty assignments.
  - `action=get_floor`: Targeted architectural polygons para sa bawat palapag (1F, 2F, 3F, 4F, RD).
  - `action=get_faculty`: Real-time presence directory ng mga propesor (e.g. Jilo Derramas sa Comp Lab 2).
